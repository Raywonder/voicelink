Build 103 completes room presence, server navigation, selected-server administration, and automatic room-media recovery.

What changed:
- Confirmed socket state now repairs the joined-room state, People list, microphone controls, and current user after reconnects or notification races.

- Selected-server screens show description, owner, contact, open/closed state, guest policy, and Administration Settings when authorized.
- VoiceOver double-tap joins the focused room. Swipe actions include Room Details, preview, sharing, and support.
- Room preview plays briefly without presenting another screen.
- Background media autoplays after a confirmed join.

- Audio Settings includes Media Playback Volume. Mute Room Output can affect room audio only or room audio plus media.

- Room messages, system announcements, transcripts, and participant updates continue while details and settings sheets are open.
