Build 98 adds room media playback and makes basic room audio available to guests as well as signed-in users.

What changed:
- Rooms can display a compact, expandable media panel. Audio streams show a small status display; video appears only when the room source contains video.
- The panel includes Play, Retry, media mute, and a source label. Room media plays locally and does not replace room conversation audio.
- Guest users can join a room and use basic microphone and output controls. Administrative actions remain server-authorized.
- Audio Settings adds a join preference: use the room default, always join muted, or always join unmuted.
- Mastodon-authenticated sessions refresh rooms after sign-in and do not silently downgrade a failed signed-in join to guest mode.
- Room transcript events continue into Live Transcripts when the server provides them.
- The beta can automatically send diagnostics on first launch and periodically while active; Settings also includes Send Diagnostics Report.
- VoiceLink server bot defaults prefer Codex/OpenClaw before local fallback models, with existing role boundaries preserved.

Please test:
- Join a room as a guest and confirm microphone and room-output controls appear after the room join completes.
- Join while signed in and confirm the same basic audio controls work, while management controls follow your server role.
- Change the join preference, leave and rejoin, and confirm the microphone starts in the selected state.
- Join a room with an audio-only background stream and confirm the expandable Room Media panel plays it.
- Join a room with a video source and confirm video appears only for that source.
- Confirm the Play or Retry control recovers media if the stream was not ready on the first attempt.
- Confirm room messages, people, room details, and audio settings sheets do not disconnect the room.
- Reproduce a Mastodon or audio issue and confirm Send Diagnostics Report is available if support asks for it.
